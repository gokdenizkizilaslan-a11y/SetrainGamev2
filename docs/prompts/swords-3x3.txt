- Arka plan sade ve koyu (her hucrede ayni ton), dis cerceve YOK.
- Yazi/rakam/harf/etiket/watermark ASLA yok.
- Sira: cell1 sol-ust ... cell9 sag-alt.
- Grid'i bozarsa 2x2 yedek grid uret (notlar dosya sonunda).

GLOBAL STYLE (her promptun başına ekle):
"single centered object per cell, dark medieval fantasy oil-painting
game icon, warm torchlit palette of muted gold-grey stone, parchment
browns, deep shadow blacks and ember-orange accents, visible brushwork,
cinematic volumetric rim light, strong readable silhouette, high
detail, fully inside its cell with a small margin, isolated on a plain
dark neutral background, no text, no letters, no numbers"

NEGATIVE (her prompta ekle):
"text, letters, numbers, typography, watermark, signature, logo, UI
labels, multiple objects in one cell, cropped subject, cut off edges,
spilling out of cell, merged cells, blurred cell borders, blurry, low
resolution, flat vector, clean 3D render, plastic, cartoon,
oversaturated, busy background, frame, border, grid lines too thick"
