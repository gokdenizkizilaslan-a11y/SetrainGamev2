================================================================
SETRA GAME - PET IMAGE PROMPTS (KREA2)
================================================================
HOW TO USE:
- 1 prompt = 1 GRID image (SQUARE 1:1). Generate, cut top-left to
  bottom-right, save each cell to its file path.
- Files go to public/images/pets/<id>.png (young/adult variants reuse
  the same art style).
- Pets show as ROUND portraits in game, so keep the beast centered
  with breathing room around it.
----------------------------------------------------------------
BASE STYLE (paint into every grid prompt):
"painterly storybook-realism creature portraits, one companion beast
per cell in a clean GRID with thin dark gaps on a dark slate
background, beast centered and front-facing with expressive eyes,
soft round vignette backdrop tinted by element (fire ember orange,
water deep blue, earth moss brown, lightning yellow white, dark
violet black, physical steel grey, frost ice blue, arcane violet,
holy gold white, shadow purple grey, nature leaf green), purely
visual wordless emblems"
================================================================
