SETRA GAME - ITEM IMAGE PROMPTS (KREA2)
================================================================
HOW TO USE:
- 1 prompt = 1 GRID image. Generate, cut top-left to bottom-right,
  save each cell to its path.
- KREA2: always SQUARE 1:1 aspect (strips too — wide canvas with
  square cells side by side).
- Files go to public/images/items/<id>.png.
- Items show as small rounded SQUARE icons in game.
- IN CELLS write only the item NAME (no .png, no path).
- FORMAT: simple numbered list in order (1. 2. 3...) — Krea puts
  them into the boxes left-to-right, top-to-bottom by itself.
- 3x3 BREAKS? Use image-prompts-2x2.txt — every 3x3 here is
  pre-split into 2x2 chunks there (same art, same names).
GRID DISCIPLINE (critical — never break this): draw EXACTLY the
cell count written in the grid line (3x3 means exactly 9 cells,
2x2 exactly 4, 1x2 strip exactly 2 side by side — no more, no
fewer). Perfect straight rows and columns, every cell the same
size square. Thick dark gap lines BETWEEN every cell and a dark
border around the whole grid — the gaps form a full cage. NO
subject may touch or cross a gap line; every icon lives FULLY
inside its own cell. No half-cut icons at the edges, no
fragments, no overflow outside the grid. Flat straight front
view, no tilt, no perspective, no rotation.
----------------------------------------------------------------
BASE STYLE (paint into every grid prompt):
"Premium game item-icon finish: single hero object per cell in
extreme close-up filling 70% of the frame, bold readable
silhouette, dramatic rim light against deep shadow, painterly oil
finish, rarity-tinted glow (common slate grey, uncommon leaf
green, rare deep blue, epic royal purple, legendary molten amber,
mythic ember red-gold), purely visual wordless emblems.
SIBLING RULE (critical): items sharing one grid must NEVER look
alike. Differentiate first by SILHOUETTE (shape, pose, angle),
then by MATERIAL (wood, iron, jewel, bone, cloth), then by glow —
no two cells the same object twice."
CUT ORDER: top-left = cell 1, left-to-right, row by row.
