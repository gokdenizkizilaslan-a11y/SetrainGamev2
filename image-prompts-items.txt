SETRA GAME - ITEM IMAGE PROMPTS (KREA2)
================================================================
HOW TO USE:
- 1 prompt = 1 GRID image (SQUARE 1:1, except 2x2 and strips noted).
  Generate, cut top-left to bottom-right, save each cell to its path.
- Files go to public/images/items/<id>.png.
- Items show as small rounded SQUARE icons in game.
----------------------------------------------------------------
BASE STYLE (paint into every grid prompt):
"League-of-Legends style square item icons, GRID with thin dark gaps
on dark slate, single centered object per cell filling most of the
frame, painterly splash-art, dramatic rim light, rarity-tinted glow
(common slate grey, uncommon leaf green, rare deep blue, epic royal
purple, legendary molten amber, mythic ember red-gold), purely visual
wordless emblems"
CUT ORDER: top-left = cell 1, left-to-right, row by row.
